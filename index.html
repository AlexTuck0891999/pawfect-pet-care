The Cat Hotel

Some guests head straight for the climbing shelves. Others prefer to spend the afternoon perfecting their nap. Our cat hotel has room for both, with private suites and one staff member for every five cats.

Private Suites

Each cat gets a private room. Cats from the same household can share a family suite. Every room has a filtered water fountain, at least one litter box, and a soft bed per cat.

Sunroom Playtime

Our large enrichment room gives guests space to run, stretch their legs, and play. With one staff member for every five cats, our team gets to know who loves a chase and who prefers to watch first.

Grooming & Guest Care

Grooming is included with every stay. A health check is included as well. If you prefer to skip the health check, let our team know at check-in.

Before your cat checks in

Please bring up-to-date vaccination records for each cat. If your cat needs vaccinations, our partner veterinary clinic can provide them at the hotel. Cats vaccinated at the hotel cannot join other cats in the enrichment room during that stay.
